## 📌 Problem Statement

Quick-service restaurants (QSRs) like Tim Hortons/Mc Donalds operate under high variability in customer demand, staffing levels, and service speed. Managers often lack real-time visibility into operational inefficiencies such as staffing misalignment, peak-hour congestion, and revenue fluctuations.


---

## 🎯 Solution

QuickServeIQ is a simulated operational intelligence system that transforms restaurant activity data into actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.



It helps managers:

- Identify peak demand hours  
- Monitor labour efficiency  
- Compare actual vs predicted sales  
- Detect operational inefficiencies  
- Make staffing decisions based on demand patterns  

---

## 🧠 Key Features

- 📊 Real-time KPI dashboard (Revenue, Customers, Wait Time, Efficiency)  
- 📈 Hourly trend analysis of sales and traffic  
- 👥 Workforce efficiency tracking  
- 🔮 Forecast vs actual sales comparison  
- ⚠️ Automated operational alerts  
- 💡 Data-driven recommendations engine  
- 🎛 Interactive filters (day/hour selection)  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Streamlit  
- Plotly  

---

## 📊 Sample Insights Generated

- Peak revenue hours identified for staffing optimisation  
- High wait-time periods correlated with customer surges  
- Labour inefficiency detected during low-demand periods  
- Revenue prediction variance analysis
